PSG Gold does not have a marketing problem. **It has already won the hardest part** — demand. What it has is a **capacity and conversion problem**, and that is a *good* problem, because it's fixable in weeks with systems, not years of brand-building.

The numbers you gave me:

| What's happening now | Number |
|---|---|
| Instagram followers | **1.1 million** |
| Average views per reel/post | **~30,000** |
| Phone calls per day | **500–1,000** |
| Unread enquiry messages sitting in the inbox | **10,000+** |
| Brand-new people messaging every day (unanswered) | **~1,000/day** |

The one sentence that runs through the whole deck:

> **"You are not short of customers. You are drowning in them — and every unanswered message is a sale walking to another shop."**

Most jewellers spend lakhs trying to *get* attention. PSG Gold already has attention almost no independent jeweller in India will ever have. The entire opportunity is to **catch, answer, and convert the demand that already exists** — and only then pour more fuel on it.
