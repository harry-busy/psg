The complete social-media growth strategy

Seven engines that build a brand from a standing start.

Not random posting — a system. Each engine feeds the next: content earns reach, reach earns followers, followers become enquiries, enquiries become customers, customers become reviews that pull in more people.

ENGINE 01

Content Pillars & Identity

A clear look and a reason to follow — not just product photos.

  • Signature visual style & brand colours
  • 5 content themes: new designs, behind-the-craft, customer joy, gold tips, festive
  • Bio, highlights & profile optimised to convert visitors
ENGINE 02

Reels Growth Engine

Reels are how a small account reaches thousands for free.

  • 4–5 scroll-stopping reels every week
  • Trending audio + jewellery hooks that get shared & saved
  • Macro shots, try-on, "which one?" formats built to go wide
ENGINE 03

Instagram SEO & Discovery

Get found when Bangalore searches for jewellery.

  • Keyword-rich captions & searchable profile
  • Local + niche hashtag strategy that actually reaches buyers
  • Geo-tags, collabs & posting-time optimisation
ENGINE 04

WhatsApp Catalog & Selling

Turn a "nice post" into a real conversation and a sale.

  • Your full collection browsable inside WhatsApp
  • Instant price / purity / availability replies
  • Click-to-WhatsApp on every post → straight to enquiry
ENGINE 05

Get-Found Locally

Own the map the moment someone searches nearby.

  • Google Business Profile fully optimised (photos, posts, offers)
  • Justdial & local listings tuned to send buyers to you
  • Rank for "jewellers in Hanumantha Nagar / Bangalore"
ENGINE 06

Reviews & Trust Engine

Your 4.0★ is gold — let's turn 40 reviews into 400.

  • Simple in-store QR that asks every happy buyer
  • Steady 5-star flow that lifts ranking & confidence
  • Customer stories & testimonials as content
ENGINE 07

Festive & Occasion Calendar

A planned year — never miss the days gold sells itself.

  • Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, Ugadi, wedding season
  • Campaign built ahead of every festival
  • Offers & broadcasts timed to peak buying
