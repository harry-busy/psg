Six fixable reasons — every one is in your data.

None of these are about how good your jewellery is. They're about the machine around it.

✕ CAPTIONS

Every post says the same thing

"925 Silver X. Available in stock. DM or visit store." No hook, no story, no desire, no reason to stop scrolling.

→ Hook-driven captions & storytelling
✕ TIMING

Posting at 11 AM

Your own peak is Sunday; strong brands like OnnMe post 6 PM. You're publishing when your buyers aren't watching.

→ Data-driven posting schedule
✕ NO OFFERS

Nothing to act on

Peers use codes & urgency ("use VAANI25"). Diyam gives no reason to buy now, so no one does.

→ Offer & campaign calendar
✕ NO WHATSAPP

"DM for details" leads nowhere

No WhatsApp Business, no catalog, no fast reply. Every interested buyer hits a dead end.

→ WhatsApp catalog + instant replies
✕ NO REACH ENGINE

Reels aren't built to travel

No hooks, trending audio, creators or shareable formats — so reels reach ~120 people, not 12,000.

→ Reel growth engine + UGC
✕ NO SYSTEM

Effort without a machine

Posting by hand, no automations, no funnel, no follow-up, no data loop. Hard work leaks out everywhere.
