# 01 · Positioning & Brand Strategy
### Diyam House of Silver

---

## 1. The positioning statement

> **For the modern Indian woman who wants sterling silver that feels precious — not cheap, not intimidating — Diyam House of Silver is the Bangalore house that makes 925 silver feel like an heirloom you can actually afford. We turn everyday metal into everyday luxury.**

The name already does heavy lifting: **"House of Silver."** That word *House* is your Tiffany-borrowed asset — it signals a maison, a lineage, a standard. Use it relentlessly. You are not "a silver shop." You are a **House**.

**One-line brand promise (put in bio + pin):**
*"925 Sterling Silver, Bangalore. Everyday luxury you can wear, gift & keep forever. ✦ Jayanagar ✦ Ships across India."*

---

## 2. The strategic problem we're solving

Your reach is growing (+40%) but engagement is flat (1.3%). Translation: **Instagram is showing your Reels to new people, and they scroll past.** Three fixable causes:

1. **No hook** — Reels open on a product with no reason to stay.
2. **No story / no save-reason** — captions are stock-availability notes, not content people bookmark or send to a sister.
3. **No consistent identity** — a viewer can't tell a Diyam Reel from any other silver shop in 0.5 seconds.

The fix is not more posting. It's **hook + story + a recognisable house look.**

---

## 3. Target audience (in priority order)

**Primary — "The Jayanagar & Bangalore woman, 22–35."** Works or studies, active on Reels, buys silver for herself and as gifts, values aesthetics and "does it look real/premium?", price-aware but will pay for quality + trust. Shops both walk-in (South Bangalore) and increasingly online.

**Secondary — "The gifter."** Buying for Rakhi, birthdays, anniversaries, return-gifts, weddings. Occasion-triggered, needs reassurance on quality (925 hallmark), packaging and delivery timelines.

**Tertiary — "Pan-India silver lover."** Discovers you through a viral Reel, buys via DM/website/ships. This is your growth engine — the auto-translation + trend audio unlock this audience.

**What they need to believe to buy:** (1) it's *real* 925 sterling, (2) it *looks* more expensive than it costs, (3) it *won't tarnish/turn skin green*, (4) *delivery & returns are safe*, (5) *this brand has taste.* Every content pillar below answers one of these.

---

## 4. Brand voice

**Warm, confident, tasteful — a stylish elder-sister who knows silver.** Not salesy-shouty. Not stiff-luxury. Premium but reachable.

- **Say:** "Meet the anklet that goes with literally everything." · "925 sterling. It'll outlive the trend." · "For the sister who steals your jewellery anyway."
- **Avoid:** "Available in stock. DM for details." (dead language), ALL-CAPS spam, over-emoji, "cheapest price!!!"
- **Signature sign-offs to build recall:** *"— House of Silver ✦"* · *"Worn in Bangalore, loved everywhere."* · *"Real silver. Real shine. Real you."*

---

## 5. Visual identity (make a Diyam Reel recognisable in 0.5s)

Consistency of *look* is what turns reach into follows. Lock these:

- **Palette:** silver/graphite + one warm accent (deep maroon or champagne) + clean off-white backgrounds. Avoid rainbow thumbnails.
- **Lighting:** soft, directional, single-source. Silver photographs best with soft window light or one softbox. No harsh flat flash.
- **Recurring motifs:** a signature surface (marble slab / raw silk / dark velvet), your hands-and-jewellery framing, and a fixed **on-screen text style** (same font, same placement — bottom third, one line).
- **Cover/thumbnail system:** every Reel cover uses the same font + a 2–4 word title so your grid reads as one designed shelf, not a jumble. This alone lifts profile-visit-to-follow rate.
- **Logo discipline:** small watermark, bottom corner, never covering the product. Hook first — brand reveal in the last 2 seconds.
- **Grid rhythm:** think in rows of 3. Alternate: Reel cover · detail shot · lifestyle. The grid is your shop window.

## 6. The TWO content engines (your hybrid model)

You asked for two reference types handled separately. Here they are as two engines you'll alternate inside the same calendar. Roughly **60% Engine A / 40% Engine B** — lead with reach, close with taste.

### Engine A — "Viral Local" (reach + sales) · ~60%
*Inspired by Giri Zever Mahal, Pukhraj, Viral Jeweller, PSG Gold.*

- **Job:** get discovered, grow followers fast, drive DMs/footfall.
- **Format:** fast, trend-audio, hook-heavy, product-forward, festive tie-ins, price/offer reveals, "which one would you pick?", satisfying close-ups, packing/unboxing.
- **Length:** 7–20 seconds, looping.
- **CTA:** "DM 'RAKHI' to order," "link in bio," "save this for later," "tag your sister."
- **KPI:** views, shares, saves, follows.

### Engine B — "House of Silver" (positioning + trust) · ~40%
*Inspired by Tiffany & Co.*

- **Job:** make Diyam feel premium and trustworthy; justify price; build the brand.
- **Format:** cinematic, slow, story/emotion-led, craftsmanship, "how we check 925 purity," heirloom/gifting stories, founder/atelier moments, styling films.
- **Length:** 15–45 seconds.
- **CTA:** soft — "Discover the collection," "The House of Silver."
- **KPI:** saves, profile visits, comments, brand sentiment, follows from quality viewers.

**Rule of thumb:** Engine A gets them in the door. Engine B makes them respect you enough to buy and stay. Never run one without the other.

## 7. Content pillars (what every Reel/post is "about")

Every piece maps to one pillar so the account feels intentional:

1. **Trust & Proof (P1)** — 925 hallmark, purity tests, anti-tarnish, real-vs-fake. *Answers "is it real?"*
2. **Styling & Versatility (P2)** — how to wear it, stacks, one piece 3 ways, GRWM, outfit pairings. *Highest-saving content.*
3. **Occasion & Gifting (P3)** — Rakhi, Independence Day, anniversaries, return gifts, packaging. *The commercial engine this month.*
4. **Product Hero / New Drop (P4)** — the satisfying close-up, the reveal, price/offer. *Engine A's bread and butter.*
5. **Brand & Behind-the-scenes (P5)** — the House story, the atelier, the owner, the packing table, customer love/UGC. *Builds trust + relatability.*

Target weekly mix: **P2 & P4 heavy for reach, P3 for sales this season, P1 & P5 for trust.**
