## PART A — How to tag a product inside a Reel (step by step)

**What it does:** puts a little shopping-bag tag on your Reel. A viewer taps it → lands on the product page → buys, without leaving Instagram. This is the single biggest "reach → sales" upgrade for Diyam.

### First, the prerequisites (one-time setup)
1. Convert to a **Professional account** → *Settings → Account type → Switch to Professional → Business.*
2. Have a **Facebook Page** linked to the Instagram account.
3. Build a **product catalog** in **Meta Commerce Manager** (business.facebook.com/commerce) — upload products manually, or connect a website platform (Shopify/WooCommerce) so it syncs automatically. Add clear titles, prices, high-res images.
4. **Submit your account for Shopping review** → *Settings → Business tools/Shopping → Sign up.* Approval usually takes **3–7 days** in India. You only do this once.
5. Once approved, turn on **product tagging** → *Settings → Shopping → Continue → select your catalog.*

> **Diyam right now:** you have 281 followers and (assumed) a website launching. Get the catalog + Shopping review started **today** — the 3–7 day approval means you want it live before Rakhi conversion peaks. Until it's approved, use the **fallback CTAs** in Part B (link sticker + DM + WhatsApp).

### Then, tagging a product in a Reel (every time you post)
1. Create/edit your Reel as normal → tap **Next**.
2. On the final share screen, tap **Tag products**.
3. Search your catalog, select the item(s), place the tag. You can tag **up to 30 products** (or one collection) per Reel.
4. Tap **Done → Share.** The Reel now shows a shopping-bag icon; taps go straight to the product page.

### Affiliate / "Add products" link option (newer, 2026)
Instagram also added an **"Add products"** option in the Reel publishing flow where you paste a **product URL** or pick from a verified catalog — viewers tap and buy in-app. Note the eligibility gate: this affiliate-style tagging needs an account that is **18+ with at least 1,000 followers.** You're at 281, so this specific option unlocks as you grow — another reason the reach-Reels in the calendar matter. Your own-catalog Shopping tags (above) don't require 1,000 followers once your shop is approved.

## PART C — The 2026 business features Diyam should switch on

### 1. Trial Reels ⭐ (test with strangers before your followers see it)
**What it is:** post a Reel that goes **only to non-followers** first. Your existing followers don't see it. You get a clean read on whether it pulls a *new* audience. 80% of creators who use Trial Reels see a reach increase with non-followers, and you can now **schedule** them in advance.
**How:** create a Reel → on the share screen, toggle **"Trial"** (Trial reel) ON → share. Check its stats after ~72 hrs; if it beats your average, **re-share it to your main feed.**
**Diyam use:** run the calendar's Day 9 and one Reel every week as a Trial first. It's a free A/B test for hooks — perfect while you're small and every new follower counts.

### 2. AI Auto-Translation & Dubbing ⭐ (this is the feature you asked about)
**What it does — plainly:** Instagram uses **Meta AI** to automatically **translate and dub** your Reel's *spoken audio* into other languages, and can even **lip-sync** it. A viewer in a different region hears your Reel **in their own language**, automatically, based on their settings. Every translated Reel is labelled *"Translated with Meta AI."* There's also caption/text/sticker translation so on-screen text adapts too.

**Languages supported (2026):** English, **Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali**, Portuguese, Spanish, plus Japanese, Korean, French, German, Italian — 14 total and growing. The South-Indian + Hindi coverage is *exactly* your Bangalore→pan-India audience.

**How you (creator) turn it on — before publishing a Reel:**
1. Record your Reel (works best when someone actually *speaks* — owner/staff on camera).
2. On the share screen tap **More options / ⋯ → "Accessibility and translation."**
3. Toggle **"Translate voices with Meta AI"** ON.
4. Optionally review the generated translation, then **Share.** Instagram auto-serves the right language per viewer.

**How a viewer controls it:** *Reel ⋯ menu → Audio and Language →* choose their language or *"Don't translate."* You don't manage this — it just works for them.

**What this means for Diyam (your exact question):** you don't make separate Reels per language. You **make one Reel in English (or Hindi), turn the toggle on, and Instagram auto-switches the language to match each viewer's region/preference.** A Chennai viewer can hear it in Tamil, a Bangalore viewer in Kannada, a Delhi viewer in Hindi — from the *same* upload. This is how you get local-feeling reach across all of India without extra production.
**Practical tip:** the "talking to camera" Reels in the calendar (Days 8, 17, 26) are your best translation candidates — give them a clear spoken script so the AI dub is clean.

### 4. Boost / Reels ads (paid reach)
Once a Reel performs organically, **Boost** it: *Reel → ⋯ / "Boost" → set goal (more profile visits / messages / website), audience, budget, duration → Boost.* Boost your **best-performing organic Reel each week**, not a fresh one — you're amplifying proven content. Great for Rakhi conversion pushes (goal = messages/website).

### 5. Broadcast Channel (one-to-many DMs)
Start a **Broadcast Channel** ("Diyam Insiders") for drops, restocks and Rakhi cut-off alerts — followers opt in, you message the whole channel. Higher open rates than feed. *Profile → New broadcast channel.*

### 6. Collabs (co-authored Reels)
Use **Collab** to co-post a Reel with an influencer/customer/complementary brand — it appears on *both* accounts' feeds and pools both audiences' engagement onto one Reel. Huge for the influencer plan in file 04. *When posting → Tag people → Invite collaborator.*

### 7. Stories tools that drive action
Turn on: **Link sticker** (traffic), **Poll / Quiz / This-or-that** (engagement signals the algorithm loves), **Countdown** (Rakhi urgency), **Add Yours** (UGC chains), **Question sticker** (collect UGC + FAQs), **Product sticker** (once Shopping is live).

### 8. Notes, Reminders & Scheduling
- **Reminder Reels:** attach a reminder to a Reel for a drop/restock so followers get pinged when it goes live.
- **Scheduling:** schedule Reels/posts natively in the Instagram app (*create → ⋯ → Schedule*) or via Meta Business Suite / a tool like Later. **Note:** native auto-publish/trending-audio has limits — for trending audio you often post manually; for original audio you can auto-publish.

### 9. Insights (read these weekly — file 02 checklist)
*Profile → Insights.* Track **shares, saves, reach from non-followers, watch time, follows-from-Reel.** These—not likes—decide virality in 2026.

## PART D — The 2026 algorithm cheat-sheet (what actually gets reach)
Pin this to your wall:
- **Ranking signals, most→least important:** watch time → completion rate → **shares** → **saves** → comments → likes. Optimise for shares & saves, *not* likes.
- **Hook in the first 3 seconds** or you lose them. Hook first, logo last.
- **Short + looping wins:** 7–15s Reels have a structural edge; keep most under 30–90s.
- **Original, made-for-Instagram content** is boosted; **reposted/watermarked/recycled** content is suppressed. Don't repost other people's Reels with your logo.
- **Trending audio** = a free distribution boost (Instagram is already pushing that sound). Save trending sounds daily from the Reels tab (tap the little arrow → "Use audio").
- **On-screen captions always on** (most people watch muted) + helps the algorithm read your topic.
- **Consistency of schedule** teaches the algorithm your cadence and raises your baseline reach — hence the daily plan.
- **Ride momentum:** when a Reel pops, post your next one within 24–48 hrs.
- **3–5 relevant hashtags + keywords** in caption (SEO), not 30 spammy ones.
