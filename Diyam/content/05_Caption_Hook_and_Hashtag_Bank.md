## F. Trending-audio routine (do this daily, 5 min)
1. Open the **Reels tab**, scroll 10–15 Reels.
2. Spot the little **↗ arrow** by the audio name = it's trending.
3. Tap → **"Save audio."**
4. Keep a running list of 10 saved sounds; match the calendar's "audio type" to a saved trend when you film.
5. Prioritise sounds with a rising (not peaked) use-count. TikTok trends hit Reels ~2 weeks later — scout there for a head start.
