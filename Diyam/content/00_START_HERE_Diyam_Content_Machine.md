## How to run the machine (daily rhythm)

**Every day you ship:**
- **1 Reel** (the growth + sales driver) — posted at your best window
- **2–4 Stories** (behind-the-scenes, polls, product links, reposts)
- On 2–3 days/week, **1 extra carousel or photo post** for depth/saves

**Best posting windows for Diyam** (from your data + 2026 best-practice): primary **8–11 AM**, secondary **7–9 PM**. Your single best slot is **Sunday ~11 AM** — put your biggest Reel of the week there.

**The 3-second rule:** every Reel must earn the next 3 seconds in its first 3 seconds. Hook first, logo later.

**After a Reel pops:** post your next Reel within 24–48 hrs to ride the algorithm's momentum.

**Weekly loop:** Mon–Sat build, **Sunday = hero Reel + week's best offer**. Review Insights every Sunday night: double down on the top format, cut the bottom one.
