# 04 · PR, Positioning & Growth Plan
### Diyam House of Silver · how the big brands do it, scaled to you

The reference brands win on three things you can copy without their budget: **a clear identity, other people's voices (influencers/UGC/press), and relentless consistency.** This plan gives you a PR + growth engine to run alongside the 30-day calendar.

---

## 1. The PR narrative (the story journalists & influencers can retell)
PR needs a *story*, not an ad. Diyam's is ready-made:

> **"The Jayanagar house making 925 sterling silver feel like an heirloom — at everyday prices."**

Angles you can pitch (each becomes a press note, an influencer brief, and a Reel):
- **"Everyday luxury"** — how real hallmarked silver became affordable for the 22–35 Bangalore woman.
- **"Made in India, made in Bangalore"** — local craft, South-India pride (strong for 15 Aug + regional press).
- **"Silver over gold"** — the cultural shift to sterling silver as the modern gifting metal (Rakhi hook).
- **Founder story** — why the House exists, the standard behind every piece.

**Assets to prepare once (your press kit):** logo pack, 8–10 hero product photos, 2 founder photos, a 100-word "About the House," the 4 angles above, and 3 signature Reels. Keep it in this folder as `Diyam_Press_Kit` when you're ready — I can build it.

---

## 2. Influencer & creator plan (borrow other people's audiences)
This is how a 281-follower account grows fast. Prioritise **micro & nano creators** — they convert better and cost little (often product-gifting only).

**Tier the outreach:**
- **Nano (1K–10K), Bangalore-based fashion/lifestyle:** gift 2–3 pieces for an honest styling Reel + Collab post. Aim for **5–8 of these in the 30 days.** Best ROI for a local silver brand.
- **Micro (10K–50K), pan-India silver/jewellery/GRWM creators:** 2–3 paid or gifted collabs timed to Rakhi and the Day 28 trend Reel.
- **Customer-creators (UGC):** anyone who buys and posts — repost every single one.

**The brief you send them (keep it simple):** hook in 3s, show the piece being worn 2–3 ways, mention "925 sterling / Jayanagar / ships pan-India," add your discount code, tag @diyamhouseofsilver, and **accept a Collab invite** so the Reel lands on your feed too (pools their audience onto your page — see file 03 §6).

**Track:** give each creator a unique code (e.g., `PRIYA10`) so you can see who actually drives orders.

## 3. UGC engine (turn customers into content)
The cheapest, most trusted content you'll ever run:
- **Insert-card in every order:** "Post your Diyam moment, tag us, get 10% off your next order." (Especially every Rakhi box.)
- **Story "Add Yours" chain:** "Show your silver stack" → resharing builds a wall of social proof.
- **Repost rule:** reshare *every* customer post to Stories within 24 hrs, and compile the best into a monthly "customer love" carousel (calendar Day 14/23).
- **Reviews to Highlights:** screenshot DM praise into a permanent *Reviews* highlight (the trust objection killer).

---

## 4. Local Bangalore / Jayanagar activation (footfall goal)
Your primary audience is local — win the neighbourhood:
- **Geo everything:** add the Jayanagar location tag + location sticker to every post/story (helps local discovery).
- **Local hashtags:** `#jayanagar #bangalorejewellery #southbangalore #nammabengaluru` on relevant posts.
- **Google Business Profile:** claim/optimise it (photos, hours, "silver jewellery Jayanagar") — half your local buyers search Maps, not Instagram.
- **In-store → online loop:** a small in-store sign — "Follow @diyamhouseofsilver for new drops & offers" + a QR to the profile. Every walk-in becomes a follower.
- **Neighbourhood collabs:** cross-promote with nearby non-competing boutiques, salons, cafés (shared Stories, a joint Rakhi corner).
- **Micro-events:** a "silver styling" or "925 purity check" pop-up afternoon — content + footfall in one.

---

## 5. Community & ORM (reputation management)
Reach is wasted if the DMs and comments drop the ball:
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** of posting — early engagement compounds reach.
- **DM response time under a few hours;** use DM automation (keyword → auto-reply with link) so no Rakhi lead goes cold.
- **Pin a helpful comment** on each Reel (price / link / "DM RAKHI") so viewers get the CTA even if they don't read the caption.
- **Save FAQ replies** (purity, shipping, returns, sizing) as quick-replies.
- **Handle any "is it real?" doubt publicly and calmly** with your hallmark proof — every good answer is trust content for the next viewer.

---

## 6. Paid amplification (small budget, big leverage)
You don't need a large budget — you need to **boost proven winners:**
- Each week, take your **top organic Reel** (by shares/saves) and **Boost it** with a small budget, goal = *messages* or *website visits*, targeting **Bangalore + women 22–35 + jewellery/gifting interests.**
- During Rakhi last-call (Days 22–30), run one small **messages campaign** to your rakhi Reel.
- Retarget: anyone who engaged/watched → show them the Rakhi cut-off Reel.

## 7. 30/60/90-day growth targets (realistic, from your baseline)
You're at 281 followers, ~1.3% engagement. Honest, achievable ladder:
- **Day 30:** 700–1,200 followers (crossing 1K unlocks affiliate product-tagging — see file 03), engagement rate 3%+, 2–3 Reels over 10K views, Rakhi orders flowing.
- **Day 60:** 2,000–3,500 followers, a repeatable "viral format," Shopping tags live, steady DM order volume.
- **Day 90:** 5,000+ followers, 1–2 breakout Reels (50K+), an influencer roster, and a self-sustaining UGC stream.
These assume daily posting + the hook/CTA discipline in this system. The lever isn't luck — it's consistency × good hooks × riding trends.
